Pure Joy on the Poomukham
The Heart of Arikil

You are miles away. We make sure they never feel it.

For Malayali sons and daughters working late in Dubai, London, Toronto, or Bengaluru, the greatest anxiety isn't money or medicine. It is knowing that Amma is drinking her evening tea in silence, or that Achan postponed his bank errand because there was nobody to walk along with him.

Loneliness is a quiet ache. In our culture, our parents are proud, dignified individuals who will never complain on Sunday phone calls. They will always say, "Everything is fine, mone. We are okay." But human beings thrive on presence—someone to listen to their stories, walk beside them, and share a hearty laugh.

"അരികിൽ ഒരാൾ ഉണ്ടെന്ന തോന്നൽ നൽകുന്ന ആശ്വാസം മറ്റൊന്നിനും നൽകാൻ കഴിയില്ല."
Independence & Dignity First
Dignity, Not Dependence

Preserving their independence in their own home.

Arikil is fundamentally different from a nursing home or clinical agency. We do not treat seniors as patients. We treat them as revered family elders. Our companions are matched carefully based on shared interests, respectful manners, and cultural harmony.

Whether it is walking down the Kalpathy heritage street, reading the morning Mathrubhumi or Malayala Manorama, or visiting the family temple during festival weeks, we restore the warmth that makes aging in Kerala beautiful.

Founder's Commitment

"Care is not a transaction. It is about becoming family."

When I founded Arikil at 29 in Palakkad, it wasn't to build a cold agency. It was born from seeing our own neighborhood aunts and uncles spend long days in quiet rooms while their children worked tirelessly in the Gulf and across the world.

Our mission is simple: to make sure your parents never feel alone, that their dignity is celebrated, and that you have complete peace of mind knowing they are safe, smiling, and cared for.
